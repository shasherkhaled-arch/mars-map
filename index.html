About this layer
Two views of the “Séítah” geologic unit. The route map shows terrain with the rover's route during its first science campaign: “Artuby” is a ridgeline along part of Séítah's southern boundary, and “Dourbes” is an abrading target on a rock in South Séítah. The mineral map shows the diversity of igneous minerals (solidified from lava or magma): olivine in red, calcium-poor pyroxene in green, calcium-rich pyroxene in blue.
